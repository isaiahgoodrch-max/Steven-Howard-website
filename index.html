The Coaching

Two things I help people do

Most people who come to me need one of these, and usually, eventually, both. We start wherever you actually are.

01

Learn to write it

The blank page, the messy draft, the chapter that won't hold together. We work on structure first, because most first-time authors are trying to cram five books into one. Then on the writing itself: making your point land, cutting what isn't earning its place, and finding the voice that sounds like you.

  • Finding and narrowing your core idea
  • Structuring your messages into a working outline
  • Developing a writing habit you'll actually keep
  • Developmental feedback as your draft takes shape
  • Line-level editing: clarity, grammar, word choice
02

Get it published

Publishing is a long series of decisions, and almost none of them are obvious the first time through. I've made all of them, 22 times over. I'll walk you through each one so your book comes out looking like it belongs on the shelf, and so people actually find it.

  • Choosing your publishing path honestly
  • Prepublication: formats, layout, trim size, ISBNs
  • Cover design decisions that don't sink you
  • Launch planning, reviews, and distribution
  • Book marketing that fits the time you actually have

Scope

What we work on

Every engagement is built around your project. These are the things that come up most.

Content structuring

Key themes, supporting messages, and substantiating detail, turned into an outline that makes the writing faster.

Developmental editing

The big-picture work. Does the argument hold? Is it in the right order? Starts early, while it's still cheap to change.

Copy editing

Grammar, spelling, word choice, consistency. The pass that happens once your draft is standing on its own.

Prepublication production

Typefaces, page layout, trim size, ISBNs, eBook and print-on-demand formats, cover design direction.

Launch strategy

Pre-launch checklist through launch week, with the steps in the order they need to happen.

Reviews & credibility

How to get honest reviews, endorsements, and the early social proof that makes a book travel.

Book marketing

Distribution options, SEO, social, press. Forty years of global branding experience, applied to your title.

Author platform

Building the audience that will be there for this book, and the next one.

The Format

How the coaching works

Straightforward, remote, and paced to your project rather than to a rigid program.

Everything happens on Zoom

Coaching is fully remote, so where you live doesn't matter. You'll get a Zoom link ahead of each session. Wherever it's useful, we share screens and work directly on your outline, your manuscript, or your launch plan while we talk.

50 minutes, one-on-one

Each session is a focused 50 minutes, just the two of us. No group calls, no recorded modules, no curriculum you work through alone. It's a real working conversation about your book, and you leave each one knowing exactly what to do next.

A cadence that fits your project

Some authors want a session every week to keep momentum. Others do better every other week, with more writing time in between. Some shift the pace as they move from drafting into production. We set your schedule when we start, and adjust it as your book and your life actually require.

Built around your manuscript

Send work ahead of a session and I'll come having read it. There's no off-the-shelf package here. The plan is shaped by what you're writing, how far along you are, and where you're getting stuck.

  1. 1

    Tell me about your book

    Send a short note about what you're writing and what you want to do with it.

  2. 2

    We talk it through

    If it's a good fit, we'll set up a first conversation about your project and your goals.

  3. 3

    We set your schedule

    Weekly, bi-weekly, or whatever the work calls for, with room to change it later.

  4. 4

    We get to work

    Session by session, from where you are now to a finished, published book.

A note on availability

I take on a limited number of one-on-one clients at a time so each person gets real attention. Coaching rates depend on the scope of your project and how often we meet. We'll cover that in our first conversation.

About

Steven Howard

Award-winning author of 22 leadership, business, and professional development books, and editor of nine more in the Project You series.

I've been writing and publishing for over 45 years, and coaching other people through it for 25. Before that I spent my corporate career in senior sales and marketing roles across Asia-Pacific, Australia, and North America. I was Regional Marketing Director for Texas Instruments Asia-Pacific, Regional Director for TIME Magazine, Global Account Director at BBDO, and VP Marketing for Citibank's Consumer Banking Group.

That background matters more than it might sound. Writing a book is one problem. Getting people to find it and read it is a completely different one. I've spent decades on both sides of that, and I bring both to every client.

I've lived in the United States, Singapore, Australia, and Mexico. I currently write from Southern California.
